Privacy Policy

Paulino AI Agents LLC · Effective November 23, 2026

Paulino AI Agents LLC ("we") provides an AI-powered front-office service that small businesses use to answer customer questions, book appointments, and send appointment-related messages by web chat, SMS, and WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Booking information you provide to a business using our service: your name, phone number, and appointment details.
  • Conversation messages you exchange with a business's AI assistant, kept so the business can review its customer conversations.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions, book and manage your appointments, and send you appointment-related messages (confirmations, reminders, and replies).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service.

Text messaging (SMS/WhatsApp)

  • You opt in by texting a business's number, or by providing your phone number when booking and agreeing to receive appointment-related messages.
  • Message frequency varies with your bookings. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in data and consent are never sold or shared with third parties.

Sharing

Your information is shared only with the business you are communicating with, and with the service providers we use to deliver messages and operate the platform (such as our messaging and cloud infrastructure providers), who may use it only to provide those services. We do not sell personal information.

Retention & security

Conversation and booking records are kept while the business uses our service and deleted on request. Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted.

Your choices

To stop messages, reply STOP. To access or delete your information, contact the business you booked with, or email us at hello@paulinoaiagents.com.
